Our story

We believe every child deserves dignity, care, and a future worth growing into.

Established in 2019 in Soweto, Hope Haven supports orphaned and vulnerable children through food access, education, and mentorship.

See Our Programmes Volunteer With Us

Mission

To provide a safe haven and opportunities for children to thrive.

Our mission is simple but powerful: create consistent care, positive learning experiences, and trusted support systems for children facing adversity.

Vision

A South Africa where every child has access to care, education, and opportunity.

We work toward a future where vulnerable children are not left behind, but supported to realize their abilities, dreams, and leadership potential.
