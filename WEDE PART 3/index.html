Our mission

We create safe spaces where children can learn, belong, and thrive.

Founded in 2019 in Soweto, Hope Haven supports orphaned and vulnerable children through food access, education support, and mentorship that restores confidence and opportunity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does my donation help? +
R50 feeds one child for a week, while R250 can cover a tutoring pack and R1,000 can support a child for a full month.
Can I volunteer if I have no experience? +
Yes. We train all volunteers and match them with tutoring, feeding or mentorship opportunities based on their skills and availability.
Where are you based? +
We are based in Soweto, and you can find our address and contact details on the Contact page.
